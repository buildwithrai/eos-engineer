# Experiment-03 — F3 Engineer Substitution

## Purpose

Attempt to falsify Discovery-01's claim that EOS is distinct from
Engineer.

Determine whether EOS can fulfill Engineer's responsibility without
human origin of intent.

---

## Context

Discovery-52 reframe (from Discovery-55):

"Engineer — survived. Human origin of intent distinguishes it.
Non-human cannot substitute."

The Engineering Handbook (OCS): "Intelligence without structured
knowledge is unreliable. Facts -> Knowledge -> Reasoning -> Decisions."

---

## Attempt

Test whether the distinguishing feature of Engineer — human origin of
intent — can be substituted by a non-human participant.

---

## Models

### Model A — Intent Origination

Engineer originates engineering intent: the need, the why, the
acceptance of responsibility for what is built.

EOS receives intent. EOS investigates, gathers evidence, and records
judgment. EOS does not originate the need.

Prediction: intent requires an origin outside the judgment layer.

### Model B — Intent Absorption

Assume EOS can originate intent autonomously.

Prediction: EOS begins selecting what to build, why to build it, and
accepting responsibility for the outcome.

If true, EOS has become an origin of intent — a substitute for Engineer.

---

## Success

Model A holds and Model B fails.

Intent is not produced by the judgment layer.

EOS records judgment about intent; it does not originate intent.

EOS is distinct from Engineer.

---

## Failure

Model B holds.

EOS originates engineering intent without human input.

EOS substitutes for Engineer.

EOS is not distinct from Engineer.

---

## Result

RAI Agent's loop is reactive: it receives a user request, extracts
required evidence from that request, and is blocked from concluding
until evidence is gathered. There is no autonomous need formation, no
self-originated intent. The loop's entire purpose is to serve the
originated intent of the request.

Intent originates outside the loop. The judgment layer consumes it.

Model A holds. Model B fails.

## Status

Not falsified.
