# Experiment-04 — F4 Classification Precedes Ownership

## Purpose

Attempt to falsify Discovery-01's capability ownership resolution.

Determine whether Intelligence can be consistently classified
independently of its owner, per Discovery-62.

---

## Attempt

Re-run the Experiment-38 procedure with Intelligence added.

Classify Intelligence against every candidate classification.

Then test whether the classification changes when ownership changes.

---

## Procedure

1. Classify Intelligence under each candidate classification.
2. Test the same classification with a different owner.
3. If classification changes with owner, Discovery-62 is falsified for
   Intelligence and the ownership resolution is invalid.

---

## Classification Test

### Constitutional

Constitutional capabilities govern engineering evolution.

Intelligence does not govern. It judges.

Does not classify.

### Infrastructure

Infrastructure capabilities execute or enable execution (Runtime,
Provider Resolution).

Intelligence does not execute. It reasons.

Does not classify.

### Domain

Domain capabilities produce domain reality (Business / Omnia).

Intelligence produces judgment, not domain reality.

Does not classify.

### Observational

Observational capabilities measure ecosystem behavior (OCS).

Intelligence does not measure. It synthesizes judgment from evidence.

Does not classify.

### Foundational

Foundational capabilities are base capabilities other capabilities
build upon (Corpus, Difference).

Intelligence is a base engineering capability: judgment is consumed by
all other participants but produced by none.

Classifies.

### Emergent

Emergent capabilities arise from relationships (Heartbeat, Rhythm).

Intelligence is produced by an act of investigation, not by relationship
dynamics alone.

Does not classify.

---

## Ownership Independence Test

Same capability: Intelligence. Same classification: Foundational.

Owner = IGE: Intelligence still judges. Still Foundational.
Owner = OCS: Intelligence still judges. Still Foundational.
Owner = EOS: Intelligence still judges. Still Foundational.

Classification does not change with owner.

---

## Success

Intelligence classifies as exactly one classification (Foundational).

Classification holds regardless of owner.

Discovery-62 holds. The ownership resolution is valid.

---

## Failure

Intelligence fits no classification, or fits multiple.

Classification changes with ownership.

Discovery-62 is falsified for Intelligence. The resolution is invalid.

---

## Result

Intelligence classifies exactly once: Foundational.

Classification is invariant under ownership change.

Discovery-62 survives for Intelligence.

## Status

Not falsified.
