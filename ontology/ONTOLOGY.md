# EOS Ontology

## Status

Derived constitutional domain.

This document establishes the beginning of the EOS ontology.

It does not amend the EOS Constitution.

It does not amend IGE.

It does not prescribe the ontology of another biome.

## Constitutional Basis

The EOS Constitution establishes the conditions under which the EOS
biome exists and remains capable of becoming.

Ontology begins downstream of that constitutional order.

The constitutional question is:

> Under what conditions can EOS exist and evolve as an IGE citizen?

The ontological question is:

> What exists, or must be distinguishable, within the EOS biome for
> that constitutional condition to remain meaningful?

## Fundamental Principle

Identity requires distinction.

Ontology therefore begins not with a predetermined taxonomy of things,
but with the distinctions necessary for a coherent EOS biome.

## Ontological Boundary

An ontological statement describes what can exist, what can be
distinguished, or how things relate within EOS.

It must not be confused with:

- constitutional authority;
- epistemological status;
- implementation structure;
- software architecture;
- database representation;
- current product behavior.

## Current Ontological Candidates

The following are candidates for further investigation:

- participant;
- actor;
- identity;
- state;
- relationship.

Their inclusion here does not establish their final definitions or
primitive status.

## Ontological Test

For every proposed ontological primitive:

> If this distinction were removed, what would become impossible to
> distinguish?

A proposed primitive must earn its place through this test.

## Open Questions

1. Is participant primitive, or is participation a relationship?
2. Is actor primitive, or is acting a capacity of something else?
3. Is identity an entity, a relationship, a projection, or a property of
   distinction?
4. Is state primitive, or is state a projection of differences?
5. Are relationships ontologically primary, or do they arise between
   already-distinguished things?
6. What distinguishes an actor from an intelligence, agent, or system?
7. Can the same entity participate at multiple constitutional scales?
8. What persists through becoming?
9. What can change without destroying identity?
10. Which distinctions are inherited from IGE and which are locally
    constituted by EOS?

## Method

Ontology proceeds through distinction, observation, investigation,
falsification, and reconciliation.

No implementation should be used as proof of an ontological primitive.

No useful software abstraction becomes ontological merely because the
implementation requires it.

The ontology remains open to revision as EOS discovers relationships
that require more precise distinctions.
